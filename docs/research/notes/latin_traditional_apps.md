# Latin and traditional Catholic prayer apps: what users praise, criticize and wish for

Method note: Reddit (reddit.com) cannot be reached by the research tools (the domain blocks the crawler), so **no Reddit threads were read**. Fish Eaters and Catholic Answers threads did not surface in search. The evidence below comes from App Store review pages (fetched directly), developer listings, and blog comment threads. Review dates are given; items before 2022 are marked **[older]**. Several third-party review aggregators (appshunter, justuseapp) returned 403 and could not be read.

## Side-by-side Latin and vernacular vs. toggle: how do people want translations shown?

### Takeaway
Users explicitly praise parallel (side-by-side) Latin and vernacular. Newer apps compete by offering every mode: Latin only, vernacular only, side by side, interlinear, or tap-a-line. Language selection that is hard to find draws complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Breviarium Meum review (2024-02-22, "Love it!! Better than all others"): praises "both English and Latin side by side" — [App Store, Breviarium Meum](https://apps.apple.com/app/id438927135)
- Introibo: TLM App review ("TLM Catholic Dream", 2025/26): "traditional Latin with its traditional English translations, side-by-side" — [App Store, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Latin Prayers (Maughold) offers Latin with side-by-side English, or either language alone, as a headline feature — [App Store, Latin Prayers](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455018998)
- Latin Prayer (latinprayer.org) instead uses interlinear translations, line-by-line under the Latin, and in the Mass you tap a line to see its translation — [App Store, Latin Prayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756076831); [latinprayer.org app page](https://latinprayer.org/en/app/)
- Introibo and Pew Pal offer Latin only, English only, or side by side — [search summary of listings, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Missale Romanum (Rafael Cereceda) review (2022-04-07): "How do I get to the English Version?" (the language switch was hard to find) — [App Store, Missale Romanum](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id638826100)
- Breviarium Meum's parallel translation comes in English, French, German, Hungarian, Italian, Polish, Spanish and more — [App Store, Breviarium Meum (CA listing)](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/breviarium-meum/id438927135)
- Universalis offers Latin "by itself or alongside the English" — reported in search results about Universalis's own description (not fetched directly) — [search result, NCRegister/Universalis context](https://www.ncregister.com/blog/why-divine-office-apps-can-t-be-beat-sometimes?amp)

### Inferences
- A toggle alone (as in Latin Rosary's La | Cz switch) is in line with some apps, but the most praised traditional apps show both languages at once. A side-by-side or interlinear option could be a wish worth considering, especially on wide screens.
- Users care that the vernacular is the *traditional* translation (e.g. Douay or classic English), not just any.

### Gaps
- No Reddit discussion could be read on toggle vs. parallel preference; no quantitative preference data found.

## Pronunciation help: ecclesiastical pronunciation guides, accent marks, audio, chant

### Takeaway
Audio is the most clearly voiced unmet need in Latin prayer apps. Reviews of text-only apps complain specifically about having no sound. Newer apps advertise audio, tap-a-word pronunciation and accent marks.

### Cited Findings
- Latin Prayers review "Disappointed" (2020-08-21) **[older]**: "No pronunciation help and definitely no audio." — [App Store, Latin Prayers](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455018998)
- Latin Prayers review "7 prayers, no audio" (2020-09-20) **[older]**: "I was hoping to hear the prayers and pray along with audio." — same source
- Latin Prayers review "Not what I expected" (2021-01-08) **[older]**: "Assumed incorrectly that this would have audio" — same source
- Latin Prayers' answer is accent marks on stressed syllables as a pronunciation aid — same source
- Latin Prayer advertises Latin audio for the Rosary and traditional prayers — [App Store, Latin Prayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756076831)
- Latinitas: Discere Orando added tap-any-word Church Latin pronunciation; Lingua Sacra cites 7,536 ecclesiastical audio recordings — [search summary of listings](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760790976); [Lingua Sacra page](https://lingua-sacra-privacy.pages.dev/)
- Introibo includes ten lessons in ecclesiastical pronunciation and 97 flashcards with phonetic pronunciation — [App Store, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Interactive Rosary in Latin sells audio of the prayers (attributed to St. John Paul II), last updated 2020, rated 2.3 from 6 ratings — [search summary](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/lifestyle/com-maddevsoftware-rosariointeractivolatin) (reviews themselves unreadable, 403)

### Inferences
- Accent marks (which Latin Rosary already keeps) are a recognized aid, but users judge them as a weaker substitute for audio.
- A cluster of 2025–26 apps (Latin Prayer, Latinitas, Lingua Sacra, Introibo) now treats audio and pronunciation as standard, raising expectations.

### Gaps
- No user evidence found on chant (Gregorian melodies for hymns and antiphons) in apps.
- The "no audio" complaints are all from 2020–21; no newer complaints found (possibly because the newer apps now have audio).

## Calendar: 1962 vs. Novus Ordo, feasts, seasonal antiphons, proper prayers of the day

### Takeaway
Calendar-aware content is central for traditional apps. Users want the correct propers and feasts and the right rubrics (1962, 1955, pre-1955). Missing propers and broken feast navigation are frequent complaints.

### Cited Findings
- iBreviary review "Still Good But Was Better" (2021-09-03) **[older]**: "it no longer offers the proper texts at all" — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- Missale Romanum review (2025-05-17): app "doesn't let you use the index proprium sanctorum option" — [App Store, Missale Romanum](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id638826100)
- Introibo lets the user choose 1962, 1955 or pre-1955 rubrics, each with its own calendar, feast ranks, liturgical colour and season — [App Store, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Latin Prayer's Mass follows the day's feast with its class and vestment colour; the Rosary picks the day's mysteries automatically — [App Store, Latin Prayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756076831); [latinprayer.org](https://latinprayer.org/en/app/)
- iMass App Store reviewer wished for explanatory notes on differences between missal versions (reported in search summary, not fetched) — [App Store, iMass](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/imass/id381131668)
- iBreviary review (2019-03-11) **[older]** asks for readings across any day of the year; another (2019-02-26) wants a Franciscan Hours option — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- iBreviary commenters (2009) **[older]**: right "about 80% of the time"; often blank just after midnight — [WDTPRS, Fr. Z](https://wdtprs.com/?p=8486)

### Inferences
- Getting today's texts right matters more to users than visuals; a wrong or empty day breaks trust quickly.
- Latin Rosary already picks the day's mysteries, the Marian antiphon and seasonal prayers; this matches what users praise in Latin Prayer and Introibo.

### Gaps
- No direct user quotes found on seasonal Marian antiphons specifically.

## Reading experience: typography, red rubrics, missal look, font size, dark mode, offline

### Takeaway
Users praise offline use and a traditional "old script" look. They complain about small fonts that can't be resized, layout bugs (text hidden by menus), poor iPad formatting and clunky, slow navigation after redesigns.

### Cited Findings
- iBreviary review "Such a blessing" (2019-03-11) **[older]**: "I like the old script style" and asks for "more of that" — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- Introibo review "Upon First Time" (2025/26): "works OFFLINE!!! GREAT!!" — [App Store, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Introibo markets a theme setting, text-size options and a parchment-style PDF export — same source
- Missale Romanum: reviewers complain of small fonts with "No options for changing or enlarging" (quoted in search summary of reviews; the page itself was 403) — [appshunter reviews](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/638826100/reviews/2)
- Breviarium Meum review "Almost there" (2019-05-14) **[older]**: "The last line is hidden by the menu on iOS" — [App Store, Breviarium Meum](https://apps.apple.com/app/id438927135)
- Breviarium Meum review (2021-02-23) **[older]**: wants "formatting for iPad screens" — same source
- iBreviary review "Second thought" (2022-01-30): "the newer version is inferior in every way to the previous version" (slower, clunky) — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- Latin Prayers markets Day and Night modes, no ads and full offline use — [App Store, Latin Prayers](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455018998)

### Inferences
- Latin Rosary's offline PWA, adjustable text size, dark mode and the Papír/Misál looks address the main reading complaints. Landscape/iPad layout is a recurring wish elsewhere.
- Redesigns that slow things down are punished in reviews; keeping things fast and stable matters.

### Gaps
- No user quotes found specifically about red rubrics.

## Learning Latin through prayer: vocabulary help, word-by-word glosses?

### Takeaway
There is clear market activity (several 2025–26 apps) around learning ecclesiastical Latin through prayer, but direct user demand quotes are thin. One review praises an app as useful for learning the Old Office.

### Cited Findings
- Breviarium Meum review "Excellent" (2018-07-24) **[older]**: "the most useful for learning the Old Office" — [App Store, Breviarium Meum](https://apps.apple.com/app/id438927135)
- Latin Prayer includes Latin vocabulary cards using spaced repetition — [App Store, Latin Prayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756076831)
- Introibo has a Learn Latin section with lessons, flashcards and Psalm/Vulgate reading exercises — [App Store, Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358)
- Latinitas: Discere Orando ("learning by praying") and Lingua Sacra are built around learning Church Latin through prayers and Scripture — [Latinitas listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760790976); [Lingua Sacra](https://lingua-sacra-privacy.pages.dev/)

### Inferences
- Developers believe learners are a sizeable audience; tap-a-word glosses or pronunciation are the common mechanism.

### Gaps
- No user reviews found that ask for word-by-word glosses specifically.

## What prayers/devotions are missing in existing apps?

### Takeaway
Wishes are mostly about breadth and completeness: specific Offices, propers, and more prayers. Small apps with few prayers are criticized.

### Cited Findings
- iBreviary review (2019-02-26) **[older]**: "The Franciscan Hours is not an option on this app" — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- Latin Prayers review (2020) **[older]**: titled "7 prayers, no audio"; another complains text is "available everywhere for free" — [App Store, Latin Prayers](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455018998)
- Competing apps now market breadth: Introibo has 67 traditional prayers, a Rosary, Stations of the Cross and a prayer-rule builder with reminders; Latin Prayer offers fifteen or twenty mysteries and an examination of conscience — [Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358); [Latin Prayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756076831)
- Laudate lists a Latin Rosary and Latin Chaplet of Divine Mercy — [search summary, Laudate listing](https://apple.co/3CU91mp)

### Inferences
- Stations of the Cross, the 15-mystery (pre-2002) rosary option, examination of conscience and reminders appear in competitors and are not in Latin Rosary.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or forum lists of "missing devotions" could be read.

## Complaints: inaccurate texts, missing diacritics, clutter, ads, paywalls, poor navigation

### Takeaway
The commonest complaints are about reliability and navigation (missing or wrong texts, broken buttons after updates, a steep learning curve). Users also value unedited, original texts and apps without ads or subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Missale Romanum review (2025-05-12): "Many buttons no longer work." after an update — [App Store, Missale Romanum](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id638826100)
- Missale Romanum review (2020-10-16) **[older]**: "It takes several tries to learn how to navigate in this app." — same source
- Missale Romanum review (2020) **[older]**: "Why is the translation of the Scripture readings different" — same source
- Breviarium Meum review (2024-02-22): "The prayers are the original prayers (unchanged, and unedited)." — [App Store, Breviarium Meum](https://apps.apple.com/app/id438927135)
- iBreviary (2009) **[older]**: "Lately the Latin has not been there at all." — [WDTPRS](https://wdtprs.com/?p=8486)
- iBreviary 2022 review: "I thought it was freezing and crashing" — [App Store, iBreviary TS Plus](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)
- Introibo review wants it to remember where the user left off in the Office; Breviarium Meum review (2019) **[older]** wants markers showing if an hour is complete — [Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358); [Breviarium Meum](https://apps.apple.com/app/id438927135)
- No-ads/no-subscription/no-account is now advertised as a selling point (Introibo, Latin Prayers, RosaryPlusHD) — [Introibo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762865358); [Latin Prayers](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455018998)
- Universalis's price drew comment: "$25 for the app!" (forum comment, older) — [search summary of WDTPRS comments](https://wdtprs.com/2009/01/iphone-application-ibreviary/)
- iBreviary aggregate rating 4.2 from about 1.1K ratings — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id422601705)

### Inferences
- Latin Rosary already keeps the user's place and works without ads or accounts, which matches recurring wishes.
- Text accuracy and sourcing are trust signals; stating sources (as Latin Rosary does) may be worth showing in the UI.

### Gaps
- No user complaints found specifically about missing diacritics. Ad complaints were not found in the reviews read.
- Divinum Officium, Missale Meum, FSSP/ICKSP apps and Preces Latinae: no user reviews could be retrieved (Missale Meum's only coverage, ccwatershed.org 2020, was paywalled).
