### Which Czech text is "the Hejčl–Sýkora"?

I found **no public scan of the New Testament volume of the 1917–1925 Bible** (*Bible česká*, Díl II). Its Old Testament volumes are public (H21 above). Two forms of Sýkora's NT are online:

- **S09/S14**: Sýkora's own first edition.
- **HS46**: the same translation, revised by Hejčl (it first appeared in 1937 as *Část prvá. Evangelia*, MZK uuid:dd9b6530-ecd7-11e4-9f58-005056827e52; the 1946 edition is the full NT).

Their wording differs in several verses (see the S09 column). I have **not** established which of the two the 1917–1925 Bible printed. HS46 is the closest to "Hejčl–Sýkora" that I could verify, so the tables give it as the main Czech reading and S09 as a variant. The user needs to choose between them.

**Capitals:** HS46 prints *Duch svatý* in lower case (Acts 2:4, Lk 1:41), and so does S09. The project's convention is "Czech capitals follow the Latin", which would give *Duchem Svatým*. That is a spelling change, which the convention allows.

## Open points

1. **Which Czech edition:** HS46 (Hejčl's revision, verified for all verses) or S09/S14 (Sýkora's first edition, verified for most of the Gospel verses). They differ in Lk 1:38, 1:42, 2:7, 2:49, 22:42, 23:46, 24:51. I found no public scan of the NT volume of the 1917–1925 *Bible česká*. NKP and MZK hold only its OT volumes publicly, and the NT may still be in an NDK-restricted scan.
2. **Latin fruit names:** none verified. I found no Latin rosary book that lists *fructus mysteriorum*. The only Latin formula I came across is a modern community site (chanttherosary.com, "…et humilitatem petamus"), which I couldn't load. The Latin names in the tables are my own suggestions, so they would need to be flagged as ours (the Conventions banner) unless a printed Latin source turns up, for example a Latin edition of Montfort or a Dominican *Manuale* in Kramerius.
3. **Czech fruit names:** no fixed list found. R94 is one 1894 witness, a set of meditations whose petitions vary from meditation to meditation. It supports *pokora, láska k bližnímu, chudoba (svatá), poslušnost, hledání Ježíše, lítost nad hříchy, trpělivost, víra, láska k Bohu, šťastná hodinka smrti*. Kramerius full-text searches for "ovoce tajemství", "užitek tajemství", "obětujeme tento desátek" and the like gave nothing usable. Nothing found for the Scourging, the Crowning, the Crucifixion, the Ascension or the Coronation.
4. **Variants needing a choice:** Presentation (Montfort's *wisdom and purity* vs the common *obedience*, which R94 also has); Finding (Montfort's *conversion* vs *piety / joy in finding Jesus*); Scourging (*mortification* vs *purity*); Crowning (*contempt of the world* vs *moral courage*); Crucifixion (*horror of sin / love of the Cross / holy death* vs *perseverance*); Pentecost (*wisdom* vs *love of God*); Assumption (*devotion to Mary* vs *happy death*); Coronation (*perseverance* vs *trust in Mary's intercession*).
5. **Latin spelling:** the VG text is drbo's unaccented Clementine. The app's style needs accents and *i* for *j* (*Iesus, eius*), which would be our own normalisation.
