# The Litany of the Holy Face — sources

`holy_face`, *Litaníæ Sanctæ Fáciei* (`LITANY_HOLY_FACE`). November 2026.

## Source

*Manual of the Archconfraternity of the Holy Face*, by the Rev. Abbé Janvier, Dean of the Metropolitan Chapter of Tours. Translated from the French by Mrs. A.-R. Bennett. Tours: The Oratory of the Holy Face. Approbation of the Archbishop of Tours, 15 April 1886. Second part, "Litanies of the Holy Face taken from the Scriptures", pp. 143–149, with the Latin and English in parallel columns.

- Scan: [virgosacrata.com, *Manual of the Holy Face*](https://www.virgosacrata.com/files/ebook/Manual-of-the-Holy-Face.pdf), PDF pp. 179–185. The litany was read from the page images.
- A transcription is on [holyfacechapel.org](https://holyfacechapel.org/manual-of-the-archconfraternity-of-the-holy-face-litanies-of-the-holy-face/). It carries OCR errors (*quresumus*, *sujus*, *cceli*, *culjus*, *abscondit*, *lacientes*), none of which is in the print.

This is a private devotion, not one of the litanies approved for public recitation. The Manual also has a second litany "composed by Sister Mary of St. Peter", in English only, which is not used here.

## Latin

As printed, with these changes of spelling and layout only:

- *Jesu*, *cujus*, *ejus*, *Juda* are spelt *Iesu*, *cuius*, *eius*, *Iuda*, and *cœlis* / *cœlesti* as *cælis* / *cælésti*, as everywhere in the app. *Exultant* is spelt *exsúltant*, *tanquam* as *tamquam*, *Exurgat* as *Exsúrgat*.
- The accents are ours.
- The book braces the response (*miserere nobis*, *libera nos, Jesu*, *te rogamus, audi nos*) beside each group of invocations. Here it is written on every line, as in the app's other litanies.
- Kept as printed: *Fili Dei, te rogámus.*, *eámdem*, *contemplári perpétuo*, and the ending *Per eúndem Dóminum nostrum*.

## English

The Manual's English column, with these changes:

- Pronouns for God are lower case.
- The responses are written on every line: *have pity on us*, *deliver us, Jesus*.
- The English prints *Jesus, in whose eyes the heavens are not pure* before *Jesus, on whose lips grace is shed*. They are put in the Latin's order.
- The petitions' *we beseech Thee*, and *We sinners beseech Thee, hear us*, become *we beseech thee, hear us* throughout. This matches the Latin's *te rogámus, audi nos*, so the English pairs with the Latin line by line. It is a change of wording, made for the layout.

## Czech

**Our own translation** of the Latin. *Tvář* is capitalized, as the Latin's *Fácies* is. The opening and the Agnus Dei are as in the app's Czech Litany of the Holy Name.

Another Czech litany, *Litanie k nejsvětějšímu obličeji Pána Ježíše*, is in *Hora Kalvarie v Jaroměřicích u Jevička na Moravě* (1884), pp. 102–104. It is public in Kramerius (MZK): book `uuid:b1578dc4-e33c-4ee1-a47a-ec6728d4a4aa`. It is a different text, and no Latin of it was found.
